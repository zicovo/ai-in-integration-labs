Aquaflux lab stack

You are browsing from inside the stack's network, so these are the same addresses your terminal uses. Not every service is running: each lab starts only what it needs.

Control layer

  • Identity provider idp.aquaflux.internal Keycloak's administration console. Sign in as admin / admin: that account lives in Keycloak's own master realm, which is why the sign-in page says Keycloak and not Aquaflux. It then opens on the aquaflux realm, with the lab's people and clients. The people (nadia, tom, els, sam, priya) sign in from the terminal, each with their own name as the password; no lab signs them in here. The certificate is trusted, so there is no warning to click past.
  • Model gateway models.aquaflux.internal:4000 LiteLLM. Serves aquaflux-default and aquaflux-alt.
  • Tool gateway tools.aquaflux.internal:4444 Written for the labs. The decision log is at /logs, the catalogue at /tools.
  • Showcase gateway showcase.aquaflux.internal IBM ContextForge, a real MCP gateway product, for the debrief. Start it with aqx showcase; it prints the sign-in.
  • Policy engine policy.aquaflux.internal:8181 Open Policy Agent. /v1/policies shows what is loaded, /v1/data/aquaflux/tools the package the gateway asks. aqx policy apply policy/ loads yours.
  • Approval service approvals.aquaflux.internal Written for the labs. The approvals currently active, with who gave them and for which connection. approve.sh records one.

Systems of record

Each opens on a read-only page of what it holds right now. Keep one open beside a lab to see what an agent's call left behind.

  • Work order management wom.aquaflux.internal Every work order it holds, with the key it was created under and who asked. The API is at /v4, described at /docs, with /.well-known/oauth-protected-resource.
  • Meter data management mdm.aquaflux.internal Monthly consumption per connection. Connection 4471 doubles in March.
  • Customer information system cis.aquaflux.internal The open dunning cases, their notes and what has happened to them. Case 4471 is overdue, with a customer note attached.

Evidence and state

  • Trace viewer traces.aquaflux.internal:16686 Jaeger. Module d-04. The audit store is beside the collector, at collector.aquaflux.internal:13133/audit/.
  • Workflow engine workflow.aquaflux.internal:8233 Temporal, development mode. Module e-03. Open a run's history here after the kill.
  • Vector engine vectors.aquaflux.internal:6333 Qdrant. Module b-01's open tier, empty until you fill it.
This page is at https://aquaflux.internal/. The lab browser runs inside the stack's network, which is why these names resolve here and not in the browser on your own machine. Start a lab with labs/stack/smoke.sh <module> in the editor's terminal. Everything is listed in labs/README.md.
