# Lead in drinking water: sampling at the tap

How samples for lead are taken at a customer's tap, and how a lead service pipe is
recognised. This is version 3 of the page, in force from June 2026; it changed the flushing
step and added the random daytime sample. Version 2 is superseded and is not used.

## Why lead samples are different

Lead in drinking water comes from the customer's own pipework or from a lead service pipe
between the main and the meter, not from the treated water. The sample therefore has to
reflect the water that has stood in the pipe, and the sampling method decides the result
more than the laboratory does.

## Flushing before the sample

For the random daytime sample, no flushing: the sample is taken from the tap as found, at a
time of day the customer was not told in advance. For the stagnation sample, flush the tap
for two minutes at full flow, close it, and take the sample after the water has stood for
thirty minutes with no water drawn anywhere in the property. Version 2 of this page asked
for a five minute flush and a sixty minute stagnation; those samples are no longer
comparable and the laboratory flags them.

## Taking the sample

Use the acid washed 250 millilitre bottle with the red cap. Fill it at a steady flow without
adjusting the tap, to the shoulder of the bottle. Do not rinse the bottle. Record the
stagnation time or "random daytime" on the label.

## Lead service pipes: identification

A lead service pipe is dull grey, soft enough to mark with a key, and gives a dull sound when
tapped. It is not magnetic. The pipe is inspected where it enters the property, at the stop
tap; a lead pipe there does not prove the whole service pipe is lead, and a copper pipe there
does not prove it is not. The finding is recorded against the connection in the customer
information system so that the sampling programme can select it.

## Reporting

Results are reported by the laboratory to the customer within ten working days. A result
above the parametric value triggers a resample and advice to the customer to flush before
use, and the connection is added to the replacement programme's list.
