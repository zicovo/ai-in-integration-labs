# Gate valves: maintenance schedule

This page sets the maintenance schedule for the resilient seated gate valves installed in the
Aquaflux distribution network, nominal sizes DN80 to DN300. It applies to valves in chambers
and to valves buried with a surface box. The schedule for pressure reducing valves is on a
separate page, and asset sheets override this page where the two disagree.

## Annual inspection

Every gate valve is inspected once every twelve months. The inspection has four steps: locate
the valve and check that the surface box or chamber cover is sound and marked, operate the
valve one full turn towards closed and back to confirm that the spindle turns freely, check
the spindle cap and the extension for corrosion, and record the number of turns to close if
the valve was exercised fully. Record the inspection against the asset in the work order
system, with the valve's asset code.

## Greasing the spindle

The spindle is greased at the same interval as the inspection, every twelve months, unless
the valve sits in a chamber that floods. Valves in flooding chambers are greased every six
months, because water displaces the grease and the spindle thread corrodes. Use the lithium
grease listed on the materials page, applied to the thread with the valve half open, and wipe
the excess so that grit does not collect on it.

## Exercising the valve

Valves that are not operated in normal service are exercised fully closed and fully open every
twenty-four months, with a technician at the downstream hydrant to confirm that the valve
seals. A valve that does not seal is reported on the fault finding page and scheduled for
repair; it is not left half closed.

## Records

Every action on this page is recorded as a completed work order of type inspection against
the valve's asset code. The asset register reads the work order system nightly; a valve with
no completed inspection in fourteen months appears on the overdue report.
