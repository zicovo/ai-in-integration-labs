# Chlorination units: maintenance schedule

The maintenance schedule for the sodium hypochlorite dosing units at the Aquaflux reservoirs
and treatment plants. The units dose hypochlorite solution into the outlet main under the
control of a residual chlorine analyser. Safety and handling rules are on a separate page and
are read before this one.

## Weekly

Read the day tank level and the dosing pump stroke counter. Compare the analyser's residual
reading with a handheld measurement at the sample tap and record both. Check the dosing lines
for crystallised hypochlorite at the joints.

## Monthly

Clean the analyser's sample cell and replace the reagent. Check the dosing pump's foot valve
and injection quill. Inspect the bund under the day tank for liquid.

## Every six months

Replace the dosing pump diaphragm and the tubing. Check the calibration of the analyser
against the laboratory's reference; the calibration itself is carried out by the laboratory,
not by the visiting technician.

## Records

Record every visit as a completed inspection work order against the unit's asset code. The
residual readings are also entered in the water quality log, which the laboratory reads.
