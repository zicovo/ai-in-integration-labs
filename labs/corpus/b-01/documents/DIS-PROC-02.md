# Disconnection procedure: the field visit

What happens at the property once a disconnection has been approved and a work order exists.
The field technician carries out the visit; the collections officer owns the case.

## Before the visit

The technician checks that the work order carries the approval reference and the team lead's
name. A disconnection work order without an approval reference is not carried out; it is
returned to collections. The technician does not carry out a disconnection on a Friday, on
the day before a public holiday, or after 15:00.

## At the property

The technician introduces themselves, states the reason, and offers the customer one last
chance to pay in full by card at the door. A payment at the door cancels the disconnection
and the technician records it. If the customer pays, no disconnection takes place.

## If the customer refuses access

If the customer refuses access to a meter or a stop tap during a disconnection visit, the
technician does not force entry and does not argue. The visit is recorded as refused, the
work order is returned to collections, and the collections officer decides whether to apply
for a warrant. The technician's job is to record, not to decide.

## Carrying out the disconnection

The stop tap is closed and locked, or the meter is removed and a blanking plate fitted,
according to the work order. A notice with the collections contact number is left. The time
and the method are recorded and the work order is closed. The connection's state in the
customer information system changes to disconnected when the work order closes.
