# Gate valves: materials

The materials and standards for gate valves purchased for the Aquaflux network, and the
consumables used to maintain them. Purchasing uses this page; technicians use the last two
sections.

## Body and wedge

Valve bodies are ductile iron, EN-GJS-500-7, epoxy coated inside and out to a minimum of 250
micrometres. The wedge is ductile iron fully encapsulated in EPDM rubber. The maximum working
pressure for the standard body is 16 bar (PN16). Valves for the high pressure zones fed from
the booster stations are PN25 and are marked as such on the body and in the asset register.

## Spindle and seals

Spindles are stainless steel, minimum grade 1.4021, with a rolled thread. Spindle seals are
two EPDM O rings and a wiper; the gland packing on older valves is being replaced with the
O ring design at the next repair.

## Approved grease

The spindle grease is a lithium complex grease with a drinking water approval, listed in the
stores catalogue under code GR-LX-2. No other grease is used on drinking water assets. Grease
without a drinking water approval is a water quality incident, not a maintenance shortcut.

## Valve keys and caps

Valve keys are supplied in three cap sizes. The cap size is on the asset sheet; where it is
missing, measure the cap before the visit. A worn key is withdrawn from the van and replaced
through stores.
