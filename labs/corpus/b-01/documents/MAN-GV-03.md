# Gate valves: fault finding

Common gate valve faults, how to recognise each, and what to do about it.

## The valve does not close fully

Symptom: the downstream hydrant keeps flowing after the counted number of turns. Usual causes:
debris on the seat, a worn wedge, or a bent spindle. Action: reopen a quarter turn and close
again to flush the seat. If it still passes, schedule a repair work order; do not leave the
valve as the isolation point for planned work.

## The spindle is stiff

Symptom: the key needs both hands and the cap deforms. Usual causes: dry thread, corrosion, or
a chamber that has flooded since the last greasing. Action: grease the spindle as on the
maintenance schedule page and operate gently. If it does not free within ten turns, stop and
schedule a repair. A snapped spindle is a larger repair than a stiff one.

## The surface box is buried or damaged

Symptom: the valve cannot be located, or the cover is broken. Action: raise a repair work
order for the civil team. Record the valve as not exercised, with the reason, so that it does
not silently disappear from the inspection round.

## Leaking gland

Symptom: water in the chamber that rises when the valve is operated. Action: tighten the
gland nut by a quarter turn while the valve is open. If it still leaks, the gland packing is
replaced under a repair work order; the valve can stay in service until then.
