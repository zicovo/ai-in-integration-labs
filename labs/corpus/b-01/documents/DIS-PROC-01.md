# Disconnection procedure: legal steps and notice periods

The steps Aquaflux takes before a household connection is disconnected for non-payment. The
procedure is set by the regional regulation on the supply of drinking water and is not
shortened for any customer. Only the collections team lead may approve a disconnection, and
only after every step below is recorded as complete.

## The reminder steps

A first reminder is sent fourteen days after the invoice due date. A second reminder, the
final notice, is sent thirty days after the first if the invoice is still unpaid, and it
states the amount, the date by which it must be paid, and the consequence. Each reminder is
recorded in the customer information system against the dunning case.

## The payment plan offer

With the final notice the customer is offered a payment plan. A customer who accepts a plan
and keeps to it is not disconnected. A plan that is broken twice returns the case to the
procedure at the step it had reached.

## The notice of disconnection

Sixty days after the final notice, if the invoice is unpaid and no plan is in force, a
notice of disconnection is sent by registered post. The customer has thirty days from that
notice before a disconnection can be carried out. In total a customer has at least one
hundred and thirty-four days from the invoice due date before the water can be cut off.

## Households that may not be disconnected

A household with a registered medical dependency on the water supply, a household with a
child under one year, and any household between 1 December and 1 March are not disconnected.
The collections officer checks the register before proposing a disconnection, and the team
lead checks it again before approving.

## The approval

The collections officer proposes the disconnection in the customer information system. The
team lead reviews the case and approves or refuses, bound to the connection number. A
disconnection work order is created only from an approved proposal.
