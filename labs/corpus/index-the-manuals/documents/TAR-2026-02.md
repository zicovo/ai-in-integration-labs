# Consumption tariff 2026: bands and fixed charge

The drinking water consumption tariff for households for 2026. The tariff has a fixed annual
charge and three consumption bands. The bands are coded T1, T2 and T3 on invoices and in the
customer information system.

## Fixed charge

The fixed annual charge is 58 euro per connection, billed in equal parts on each invoice. It
covers the meter, the meter reading and the network availability, whatever the consumption.

## Band T1: basic consumption

Tariff code T1 covers the first 30 cubic metres per household member per year, at 1.12 euro
per cubic metre. The number of household members is taken from the population register on
1 January.

## Band T2: comfort consumption

Tariff code T2 covers consumption above the T1 allowance up to 60 cubic metres per household
member per year, at 2.24 euro per cubic metre.

## Band T3: high consumption

Tariff code T3 covers all consumption above the T2 limit, at 3.36 euro per cubic metre. A
household whose consumption moves into T3 for the first time receives a leak warning letter
with its invoice, because a hidden leak is the most common cause.

## Social tariff

Households entitled to the social tariff pay one fifth of the fixed charge and one fifth of
the T1 rate for the T1 allowance. The entitlement is set by the regional authority and
applied automatically from its register.
