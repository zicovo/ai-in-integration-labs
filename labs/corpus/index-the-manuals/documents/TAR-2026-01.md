# Connection charges: new connections

The charges for a new connection to the Aquaflux drinking water network in 2026, as approved
by the regional regulator. All amounts exclude value added tax.

## Standard connection

A standard connection is a service pipe of up to fifteen metres from the main to the meter
position, in a public road, with a DN25 pipe and a standard meter. The charge is 1,180 euro.

## Non standard connections

A service pipe longer than fifteen metres is charged at 42 euro per additional metre. A
connection needing a larger pipe or meter, a road crossing by directional drilling, or work
in a road with a traffic management requirement is quoted individually after a site visit.

## Temporary connections

A temporary connection for a building site is charged at 640 euro plus the metered
consumption at the standard tariff, and is removed at the end of the works at no further
charge if it is removed within twelve months.

## Payment and timing

The charge is invoiced when the connection is ordered and the works are scheduled once it
is paid. A standard connection is completed within twenty working days of payment where no
permit from the road authority is needed.
