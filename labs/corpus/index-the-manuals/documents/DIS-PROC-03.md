# Disconnection procedure: reconnection and fees

How a disconnected connection is restored, and what it costs the customer. The reconnection
fee is set in the tariff, not in this procedure; this page says when it applies.

## Conditions for reconnection

The connection is reconnected when the outstanding amount is paid in full or a payment plan
is agreed and its first instalment is paid, and the reconnection fee is paid or added to the
plan. The collections officer confirms both in the customer information system and creates
the reconnection work order. No approval by the team lead is needed for a reconnection.

## Timing

A reconnection is carried out within one working day of the conditions being met, and on
the same day where the customer pays before 12:00. Reconnections take priority over planned
work in the field schedule.

## The reconnection fee

The fee applies to every reconnection after a disconnection for arrears, and it is the amount
stated in the current tariff page on reconnection and administrative charges. It is not
charged where the disconnection is found to have been carried out in error, and it is
refunded with an apology where the error was Aquaflux's.

## The field visit

The technician removes the lock or refits the meter, opens the stop tap slowly, checks for
leaks at the meter, and records the meter reading at reconnection. The reading is the start
of the new billing period.
