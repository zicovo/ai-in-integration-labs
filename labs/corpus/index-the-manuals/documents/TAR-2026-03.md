# Reconnection fee and administrative charges

The administrative charges Aquaflux applies in 2026, as approved by the regional regulator.
All amounts exclude value added tax. When one of these charges applies is set out in the
relevant procedure; this page sets the amounts.

## Reconnection fee

The fee for reconnection after a disconnection for arrears is 175 euro. It covers the field
visit to restore the connection and the administration of the case. It is charged once per
disconnection, whatever the number of visits needed to reconnect.

## Reminder charges

No charge is made for the first reminder. The final notice carries a charge of 7.50 euro and
the notice of disconnection by registered post a charge of 15 euro, both added to the
outstanding amount.

## Meter test at the customer's request

A customer who disputes a reading may ask for the meter to be tested at an accredited
laboratory. The charge is 95 euro, refunded in full if the meter is found to read more than
the permitted error in Aquaflux's favour.

## Duplicate invoices and statements

Duplicate paper invoices are 2.50 euro each; the same documents are free in the customer's
online account.
