# Booster pumps: maintenance schedule

The maintenance schedule for the booster pump sets in the Aquaflux pumping stations. A pump
set is two or three centrifugal pumps on a common manifold with a variable speed drive per
pump. Alarms and fault finding are on a separate page.

## Monthly

Read the run hours per pump and rotate the duty pump if the station controller has not done
so. Check the manifold and the pump glands for leaks. Listen for bearing noise. Record the
suction and discharge pressures against the station's normal values.

## Every six months

Check the mechanical seal for weeping, check the coupling alignment, and clean the drive
cabinet filters. Test the low suction pressure cut out by closing the suction valve slowly
with the pump running and confirming that the drive stops the pump.

## Every twelve months

Replace the drive cabinet filters, check the bearing temperature under load with the
handheld thermometer, and check the non return valve on each pump for chatter. Vibration
measurements are taken by the maintenance engineer, not by the visiting technician.

## After any repair

A pressure test is required after any repair that opened the pump casing or the manifold.
The test is described on the pressure testing page; do not return the pump to duty until it
has passed and the result is recorded.
